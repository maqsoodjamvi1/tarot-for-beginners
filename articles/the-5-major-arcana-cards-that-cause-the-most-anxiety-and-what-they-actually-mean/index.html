If you are new to tarot, flipping through a deck can feel a bit intimidating. While most cards carry nuanced, complex messages, a few specific Major Arcana cards have earned a dramatic reputation. When one of these pops up in a spread, it is easy to assume the worst.

I remember the first time I pulled the Tower card during a routine Tuesday reading about my career. My stomach dropped, and I seriously considered closing the deck and walking away for good. Years later, as a practicing reader, I see that same panic in clients all the time.

The truth is, these cards rarely mean what beginners fear they mean. Instead of predicting doom, they act as flashing warning signs or urgent calls to wake up and pay attention. Here are the five Major Arcana cards that cause the most anxiety, and what they are actually trying to tell you about your future.

1. The Tower

Let’s get the big one out of the way first. The Tower shows a stone fortress struck by lightning, with figures tumbling headfirst into the void. It looks terrifying. In practical terms, the Tower represents a sudden, unshakeable disruption. It is the breakup you didn’t see coming, the company layoffs announced on a Friday morning, or the realization that a long-held belief of yours is completely false.

Here is the reframe: the Tower only destroys things that were built on shaky foundations. If your job was already making you miserable, the layoff is a violent push toward something better. If your relationship was fundamentally broken, the sudden ending stops you from wasting another five years. The Tower clears out the rubble so you can finally build something authentic.

2. Death

Pop culture has done a real number on the Death card. Seeing a skeleton in heavy armor riding a pale horse makes people instantly think of physical mortality. Take a deep breath: Death almost never means literal death in a standard reading.

Instead, Death represents a profound, necessary transition. It is the end of a chapter. Think of graduating college, moving out of your hometown, or finally breaking a toxic cycle of people-pleasing. Death tells you that a specific phase of your life has run its course, and clinging to it will only cause unnecessary suffering. You cannot step into your next evolution until you allow the old version of yourself to fade away.

3. The Devil

The Devil card features a horned creature holding two human figures captive by loose chains around their necks. The immediate fear is that you are trapped by outside forces, cursed, or doomed to repeat a miserable pattern forever.

Look closer at the card, though: the chains are loose enough that the figures could slip them off their necks anytime they wanted to. The Devil actually points to self-imposed restriction, unhealthy attachments, and bad habits. It shows up when you are staying in a bad situation because it is familiar, or numbing out with habits that drain your energy. It is a mirror, asking you to look honestly at where you are giving away your personal power for short-term comfort.

4. The Ten of Swords (A Major Arcana Cousin)

While technically part of the Minor Arcana, the Ten of Swords causes just as much panic as any Major Arcana card. It depicts a figure lying face down with ten swords piercing their back. It looks like a murder scene.

In real life, this card points to hitting rock bottom. It represents burnout, betrayal, or the feeling that you simply cannot take one more hit. But there is a hidden mercy in this card: look at the horizon. The sky is clearing. When the Ten of Swords appears, it means the absolute worst has already happened. You survived the blow. The only direction left to go is up, and you can finally stop bracing for impact.

5. The Hanged Man

The Hanged Man shows a figure dangling upside down by one ankle, looking entirely peaceful with a glowing halo around his head. For a culture obsessed with hustle, productivity, and forward momentum, this card is pure anxiety. It means total, forced suspension.

When the Hanged Man appears, your plans are going to stall. Job applications will go unanswered, negotiations will drag out, and life will refuse to move forward. This card tells you that forcing action right now will backfire. Instead, you need to change your perspective. Use this waiting period to look at your situation from a completely different angle, rest your nervous system, and let go of the need to control the timeline.

Working with these intense cards gets easier the more you see them in context. When a scary card lands in your spread, try taking a step back before you panic. Ask yourself what structure needs updating, what habit needs breaking, or what area of your life is simply asking you to pause and rest.
